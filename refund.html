How to request a refund

For a Paddle-processed purchase, use the support or transaction-management link on your Paddle receipt, or visit paddle.net to request a refund. Include your order reference, not card numbers or passwords. If a report is missing, inaccessible, defective or materially different from its stated scope, you may also notify feedhealth@qianwuai.com so we can investigate delivery promptly; refund decisions and payment reversals are processed by Paddle.

Verified Feed Diagnosis is a single-purchase digital report with no automatic renewal; there is no subscription to cancel. If a refund is granted, access to the related paid report may be revoked as permitted by law and Paddle's terms.

For country-specific withdrawal periods, defective or misdescribed digital content, and discretionary refunds, please consult Paddle's current policy. Where that policy, mandatory law and any additional seller rights differ, mandatory and more protective applicable rights prevail.
