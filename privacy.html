Privacy and data scope

The free Feed Health check operates inside your browser using publicly accessible Shopify product data or non-sensitive sample JSON that you choose to paste. It does not request Shopify Admin access. Product analysis happens in your browser, and this static website does not store your store URL, private credentials or product payloads.

We do not run third-party ad pixels or use persistent browser tracking. Our hosting and DNS providers may process normal technical HTTP request information, including IP address, timestamps and browser metadata, to provide the site and prevent abuse.

If you choose to email us, you decide what to send. We may use your business contact details and information you supply to respond to your request. Do not send customer data, private order records, access tokens or payment information. For a privacy enquiry, email feedhealth@qianwuai.com.
