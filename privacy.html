Privacy and data scope

Current free checker

The free product-data scan operates inside the visitor's browser, using public Shopify product data or non-sensitive sample JSON the visitor chooses to paste. The current free checker does not request Shopify Admin access or transmit a visitor's store URL or product payload to a Feed Health server. The free tool does not use advertising pixels or persistent browser tracking. Hosting and DNS providers may process standard technical request data (such as IP address, timestamps and user-agent) for delivery and security.

Planned paid diagnosis (not yet live)

When paid checkout and delivery are approved and enabled, we will request the buyer's explicit consent, a delivery contact, and a limited public or authorized product-data snapshot. The original secured Group service will use that evidence to generate an AI diagnosis, associate it with a trusted Paddle transaction, provide access-controlled delivery and handle technical retries and support. Paid report data will not be placed in public URL parameters or payment-provider custom metadata. The planned report-access token expires after 30 days; transactional or legal records may be retained separately as required. Any changes to actual retention or processors will be reflected here before launch.

Payment processing and your choices

When live, Paddle will process checkout and payment details under its privacy disclosures. Feed Health does not ask for or store payment-card numbers. You can decline paid data processing by not purchasing and continue using the free checker. If you contact us, we use voluntarily submitted business contact information to respond to your request. Do not send buyer/customer PII, credentials, payment details or private financial records.

Privacy enquiries

To ask about data access, correction, deletion, processing or retention, contact feedhealth@qianwuai.com. Applicable legal rights and retention obligations continue to apply.
