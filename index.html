Scoring method

Score is the weighted completeness rate across title, description, image, image alt text, vendor, product type, SKU, and positive price checks. Barcode/GTIN coverage is shown as an opportunity and does not reduce the score.

Coverage boundary

What this scan can — and cannot — verify

Checked now: public Shopify product-data completeness and consistency available to this browser.

Not checked: Merchant Center disapprovals, policy flags, shipping settings, account diagnostics, private inventory, or authenticated channel status.
